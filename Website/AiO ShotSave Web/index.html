A shelf that remembers

Shots are numbered #1, #2 and so on. Reopen the app and your latest shots are back in the shelf. Choose how many in Settings: Off, 5, 10 or 20.

Copy text from any shot

Box any text and press 5. Every line shows up in a small panel, ready to copy. Works offline, and on Windows it reads Vietnamese with full accents.

A storyboard in 3 seconds

Box a moving area and press S. Three seconds later you have six numbered shots in one strip, kept in the Storyboard shelf. Remove any shot, then drag the whole strip into any app.

Record any area as video

Box an area and press R. Recording runs until you hit Stop, up to 5 minutes. The MP4 waits in the Video shelf: pick With sound or No sound, then drag the row into your chat or any other app. On Windows it records your computer's sound too.

A shelf that tucks itself away

Leave the shelf alone for 5 seconds and it folds into a small round button in the corner, showing your latest shot and how many are waiting. Click the button and the shelf opens right where it was.

Number your steps

Press 6 and click on the shot: a badge with 1, then 2, then 3. Delete one in the middle and the rest renumber themselves.

Pen and highlighter

Press 7 to draw freehand, 8 to highlight what matters. Hold Shift for a straight line.

Scrolling capture

Box the part of the page that scrolls and press 9. Scroll down with your mouse and the app stitches what passes by into one long image. Click Done to save it.

Turn a recording into a GIF

In the Video shelf, click GIF on any recording under 60 seconds. A few seconds later the GIF is ready to drag into a chat or any other app, just like the video.

One drag away

Questions

What exactly do I pay?

$14.99, once. That covers every feature and every update we release after, with nothing to renew.

Do I pay again for new versions?

No. Every new version is free for the license you already own.

How many computers can I use it on?

Two, for example one at work and one at home. Changing computers? Press Deactivate on this computer in Settings on the old one, then activate on the new one.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes, as a beta for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The first time you open it, macOS may ask you to confirm in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
