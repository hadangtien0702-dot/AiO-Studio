Questions

Does it work with DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut?

No. Every tool is a Premiere Pro panel and lives inside Premiere.

Is there a Mac version?

Windows first. Mac is planned: the panels themselves run there, but the speech and video engines are still Windows builds.

Does my footage get uploaded anywhere?

No. Speech recognition and video processing run on your machine. The only tool that uses the internet is Video Download, for the link you paste.

Do I need admin rights to install?

No. The installer runs under your own user account and turns on the one Premiere setting the panels need.

When does it open, and what will it cost?
