LUU Y KHI CAI DE
----------------
App dang mo thi cai de se khoi dong lai app -> KHAY dang co
anh se trong lai (FILE ANH KHONG MAT - van nam trong thu muc
luu, bam nut thu muc tren khay la thay).

CAI DAT
-------
1. Chay file: AiO-Shot-and-Save-Setup-0.3.13.exe
2. Bo cai MOT CU BAM (khong hoi gi), cai cho rieng nguoi dung
   (khong can quyen Admin). Cai de len ban cu duoc luon.
3. Loi tat nam o Start Menu + Desktop: "AiO Shot & Save".

!! CANH BAO WINDOWS SMARTSCREEN !!
Ban nay CHUA ky so (code-signing) nen lan dau chay Windows co
the hien "Windows protected your PC":
  -> bam "More info" -> "Run anyway".

DUNG NHU THE NAO
----------------
- Phim tat chup mac dinh: Ctrl + Shift + S (doi duoc trong Cai dat)
- Bam phim tat -> keo chon vung (keo VAT NGANG 2 man hinh cung duoc)
- Chon vung xong: ve khung / mui ten (7 mau), roi:
    Enter hoac nut V  = luu vao khay
    Ctrl + C          = luu vao khay + copy vao clipboard
    Bam ra ngoai vung = chon lai vung khac
    Esc               = huy
- Anh nam trong KHAY (goc man hinh):
    Bam thumbnail     = ghim anh noi len man hinh
    KEO thumbnail     = tha file vao Premiere / Zalo / Messenger...
    LAN con lan chuot = cuon day anh
    Re chuot          = xem kich thuoc + dung luong
- Cai dat (chuot phai icon khay he thong -> Cai dat):
    phim tat · dinh dang JPEG/PNG · chat luong · kieu khay
    (ngang/doc) · thu muc luu · ngon ngu VI/EN

ANH LUU O DAU
-------------
Mac dinh: thu muc "Anh chup" ngay canh cho cai app
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\aio-shot-and-save\Anh chup).
Doi duoc trong Cai dat -> Thu muc luu anh.

GO CAI DAT
----------
Settings Windows -> Apps -> AiO Shot & Save -> Uninstall.
(Anh da chup KHONG bi xoa khi go app.)
