MOI SO VOI 0.4.0 (31/08)
------------------------
- VE CHU LEN ANH (phim 3): chu dam, co HOP NEN toi bo goc, doc
  duoc tren moi anh. Phim 1 = khung, 2 = mui ten, 3 = chu.
- TREN ANH GHIM: re chuot len anh la hien 3 nut khung / mui
  ten / chu, bam la ve tiep. Phim 1/2/3 van dung duoc.
- KEO TO KHAY: re chuot len khay, keo tay nam goc TREN-TRAI.
  To ra = THAY NHIEU ANH HON (ngang them hang, doc them cot),
  o anh giu nguyen co. Nho co rieng cho khay ngang / doc.
- CHUP NHANH GAP 2,7 LAN (1,24s -> 0,47s). Man toi di muot.
- VIDEO (YouTube / Facebook / TikTok) KHONG CON DEN khi
  khoanh: app chup TRUOC roi moi phu lop mo, hinh dung yen
  hien ngay (nhu Lightshot). Overlay hien sau ~0,6s.
- KEO-THA RA APP KHAC chay voi MOI thu muc luu, ke ca ten co
  dau '&', dau cach, tieng Viet (truoc: Chrome / Lark / Teams
  / Zalo / Messenger nhan file rong). Da do that: Premiere,
  Photoshop, Figma, Explorer, Claude, Messenger, Lark, Teams,
  Zalo web, Chrome.
- THU MUC LUU ANH MAC DINH DOI: %LOCALAPPDATA%\shotandsave
  (nam NGOAI thu muc cai -> cap nhat app KHONG mat anh).
  Ten file: shotandsave-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS-mmm.jpg
- Cai dat: kieu khay ghi ro "Ngang / Doc".

CAI DAT
-------
1. Chay: AiO-Shot-and-Save-Setup-0.4.17.exe
2. Bo cai MOT CU BAM, per-user (khong can Admin). Cai de len
   ban cu duoc luon; phim tat + cai dat cua ban GIU NGUYEN.
3. Loi tat: Start Menu + Desktop "AiO Shot & Save".

!! SMARTSCREEN: ban chua ky so, lan dau chay Windows co the
chan -> "More info" -> "Run anyway".

!! CAI DE TU BAN <= 0.4.5: anh cu nam trong thu muc cai
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\aio-shot-and-save\Anh chup) SE BI
XOA khi cai de. Chep thu muc do ra ngoai TRUOC khi cai.
Tu 0.4.6 tro di anh da nam ngoai thu muc cai, cai de an toan.

DUNG NHU THE NAO
----------------
- Phim tat chup mac dinh: Ctrl + Shift + S (doi trong Cai dat)
- Bam phim tat -> hinh dung yen hien -> keo chon vung (vat 2
  man cung duoc)
- Chon vung xong: ve khung (1) / mui ten (2) / chu (3),
  7 mau, roi:
    Enter hoac nut V  = luu vao khay
    Ctrl + C          = luu + copy clipboard
    Esc               = huy
- Khay (goc man hinh):
    Bam thumbnail     = ghim anh len man hinh
    KEO thumbnail     = tha file vao Premiere / Zalo / Mess...
    LAN con lan chuot = cuon day anh
    Keo goc tren-trai = phong to khay (thay nhieu anh hon)
- Anh ghim: re chuot -> 3 nut ve; keo ANH = tha ra app khac;
  keo THANH TREN = di chuyen; Ctrl + lan chuot = do mo.
- Cai dat (chuot phai icon khay he thong): phim tat ·
  JPEG/PNG · chat luong · kieu khay Ngang/Doc · thu muc · VI/EN

ANH LUU O: %LOCALAPPDATA%\shotandsave (doi duoc trong Cai dat)
GO: Settings Windows -> Apps -> AiO Shot & Save -> Uninstall
(anh da chup KHONG bi xoa).
