CAI DAT
-------
1. Chay: AiO-Shot-and-Save-Setup-0.4.0.exe
2. Bo cai MOT CU BAM, per-user (khong can Admin). Cai de len
   ban cu duoc luon; phim tat + cai dat cua ban GIU NGUYEN.
3. Loi tat: Start Menu + Desktop "AiO Shot & Save".

!! SMARTSCREEN: ban chua ky so, lan dau chay Windows co the
chan -> "More info" -> "Run anyway".

trong lai (FILE ANH KHONG MAT - van trong thu muc luu).

DUNG NHU THE NAO
----------------
- Phim tat chup mac dinh: Ctrl + Shift + S (doi trong Cai dat)
- Bam phim tat -> keo chon vung (vat 2 man cung duoc)
- Chon vung xong: ve khung / mui ten (7 mau), roi:
    Enter hoac nut V  = luu vao khay
    Ctrl + C          = luu + copy clipboard
    Bam ra ngoai vung = chon lai
    Esc               = huy
- Khay (goc man hinh):
    Bam thumbnail     = ghim anh len man hinh (ve tiep duoc)
    KEO thumbnail     = tha file vao Premiere / Zalo / Mess...
    LAN con lan chuot = cuon day anh
- Cai dat (chuot phai icon khay he thong): phim tat ·
  JPEG/PNG · chat luong · kieu khay ngang/doc · thu muc · VI/EN

ANH LUU O: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\aio-shot-and-save\Anh chup
(doi duoc trong Cai dat)
GO: Settings Windows -> Apps -> AiO Shot & Save -> Uninstall
(anh da chup KHONG bi xoa).
