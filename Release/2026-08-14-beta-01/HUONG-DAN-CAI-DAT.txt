AiO Studio BETA - bo 3 panel cho Adobe Premiere Pro (Windows)
  - AiO Autocut        : do khoang lang -> cat + don clip
  - AiO Asset Manager  : kho asset - tim, nghe thu, keo vao timeline
  - AiO Power Bins     : Brand Kit hien o MOI project Premiere

YEU CAU
  - Windows 10/11
  - Adobe Premiere Pro (da kiem chung tren Beta 26.5 / CEP 12)
  - KHONG can cai them gi: khong Python, khong Node, khong FFmpeg
    (da nam san trong bo), khong can quyen Admin.

TRUOC KHI GIAI NEN - LAM BUOC NAY TRUOC CHO DO VUONG
  File tai tu mang (Drive, Gmail...) bi Windows danh dau 'tu Internet',
  bam dup CAI-DAT.bat se hien bang xanh chan lai.
  Cach tranh: chuot phai file ZIP -> Properties -> tick 'Unblock' -> OK,
  roi moi giai nen.
  Neu lo giai nen truoc: khi bang xanh hien ra bam 'More info' ->
  'Run anyway'. Do chi la canh bao cho file tai tu mang, khong sao ca.

CACH CAI
  1. Dong han Premiere Pro.
  2. Bam dup CAI-DAT.bat.
  3. Mo Premiere -> Window -> Extensions -> chon panel muon dung.

DOI NGON NGU / SWITCH LANGUAGE
  Moi panel co nut VI / EN o thanh tren cung - bam la doi ca bo.
  Every panel has a VI / EN button in its top bar - one click switches
  the whole suite.

GO CAI DAT
  Xoa 3 thu muc trong %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\ :
    com.aiostudio.autocut
    com.aiostudio.assetmanager
    com.aiostudio.powerbin
  va thu muc kho dung chung %APPDATA%\AiOStudio\bin\win64
