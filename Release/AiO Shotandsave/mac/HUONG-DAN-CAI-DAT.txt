AiO Shot & Save 0.5.0 cho macOS - 15/09/2026

Chon dung file theo may:
  - Mac chip M (M1/M2/M3/M4): AiO-Shot-and-Save-0.5.0-mac-arm64.dmg
  - Mac chip Intel:           AiO-Shot-and-Save-0.5.0-mac-x64.dmg
  (Khong biet chip gi: bam logo Apple -> About This Mac.)

1. Mo file .dmg, keo "AiO Shot & Save" vao thu muc Applications.
2. Lan dau mo, macOS bao "Apple could not verify ... is free of malware"
   (app chua ky so, binh thuong). Bam Done, roi vao System Settings ->
   Privacy & Security -> keo xuong cuoi -> "Open Anyway" -> nhap mat khau.
   (Hoac mo Terminal, dan:  xattr -cr "/Applications/AiO Shot & Save.app" )
3. macOS hoi quyen Screen Recording -> Allow, roi mo lai app.
4. Xong. Phim tat chup: Cmd + Shift + S (doi trong Cai dat).
